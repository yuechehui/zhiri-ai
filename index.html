About the I Ching

The I Ching is a traditional Chinese classic that explores change, balance and decision-making.

This page performs a real three-coin cast: three coins are tossed for each of the six lines, from the bottom line up, and the resulting hexagram is matched against the 64 hexagrams of the King Wen sequence. Changing lines point to a second, changed hexagram.

This is a reflection tool, not a prediction of future events. Use it to think about a situation from a different angle.
